Privacy Policy for Arrow Escape

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Arrow Escape ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our"). We take user privacy seriously. This document clearly describes how data is handled when you download and play our game.

1. Target Audience & Children's Privacy

Arrow Escape is designed and intended for a general audience aged 13 and above. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. standard Google AdMob rules apply and the application does not participate in the Google Play Designed for Families programme.

2. Information We Collect Automatically

To deliver a smooth puzzle experience, analyze stability, and show relevant ads, third-party SDKs integrated into the game collect certain technical signals:

  • Device Identifiers & Advertising ID: Used by Google AdMob for ad delivery, fraud detection, and frequency capping.
  • App Activity & Gameplay Telemetry: Level start, level clear, hints used, and progression stats gathered anonymously via Firebase Analytics.
  • Crash Logs & Performance Metrics: Diagnostic stack traces and device model information gathered via Firebase Crashlytics to resolve crashes and maintain a 99.5%+ crash-free session rate.

3. Advertising and Google UMP Consent

The game utilizes Google AdMob to display ads (Banners, Interstitials, and Rewarded Ads). For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the application integrates Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. Users can review or modify their consent preferences at any time via the "Privacy Options" button in the Settings screen.

4. In-App Purchases

In-app purchases (such as "Remove Ads" and Coin Packs) are processed exclusively through Google Play Billing. We do not store, process, or have access to your credit card or financial details. Purchase entitlements are securely stored on your device.

5. Data Security & Retention

All communication with backend services is encrypted using HTTPS / TLS. Game progress, unlocked skins, and coin balances are saved locally on your device storage via encrypted key-value preferences.
