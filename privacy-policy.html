Privacy Policy for Arrow Escape

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Arrow Escape: Line Puzzle Game (package name: com.arrowescape.linepuzzle), developed and published by Abhijeet Pratap Dhaka ("the Developer", "we", "us", or "our"). We take user privacy seriously. This document clearly describes what data is collected when you download and play our game, how it is used, and with whom it is shared.

By downloading, installing, or using Arrow Escape you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app.

1. Target Audience & Children's Privacy

Arrow Escape is designed for a general audience aged 13 and above. The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. It does not participate in the Google Play "Designed for Families" programme. Standard Google AdMob policies apply.

If we learn that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided information may contact us at the email listed below.

2. Information We Collect

Arrow Escape does not require you to create an account, enter your name, email, phone number, or any other personal information to play. We do not collect photos, contacts, location, camera, microphone, files, or health data.

The following data is collected automatically through third-party SDKs integrated into the app:

Data type Collected by Purpose
Advertising ID (AAID) Google AdMob Ad delivery, fraud detection, frequency capping
App interactions (level start, level clear, hints used, ads watched) Google Firebase Analytics Understanding gameplay patterns, tuning difficulty curves
Crash logs & diagnostics (stack traces, device model, OS version) Google Firebase Crashlytics Identifying and fixing crashes to maintain stability
Anonymous user ID Google Firebase Authentication Leaderboard scores and challenge progress (no personal info required)
Remote configuration values Google Firebase Remote Config Adjusting ad frequency and game balance without an app update

Locally stored data: Your game progress (levels completed, stars, coins, unlocked skins, streak, settings preferences) is saved on your device using encrypted key-value storage (@capacitor/preferences). This data is never transmitted to our servers.

3. How We Use the Data

  • To serve ads: Display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads via Google AdMob.
  • To improve the game: Analyse anonymous gameplay telemetry to adjust difficulty, fix bugs, and plan new content.
  • To maintain stability: Monitor crash-free session rates and resolve issues quickly.
  • To power leaderboards and challenges: Store anonymous scores in Firebase so players can compare results.
  • To schedule local reminders: If you opt in, the app uses on-device local notifications to remind you of the daily puzzle. No data leaves your device for this.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the following third-party service providers, solely for the purposes described above:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Authentication, Remote Config) — Firebase Privacy & Security
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) — Google Privacy Policy

These services may collect device identifiers and technical data in accordance with their own privacy policies linked above. We encourage you to review them.

We do not share data with any data brokers, social networks, or other third parties beyond those listed.

5. Advertising & Consent (GDPR / UMP)

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app integrates Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) to collect consent before serving personalised ads or collecting identifiers.

You can review or change your consent preferences at any time through the "Privacy Options" button in the app's Settings screen.

If consent is not granted, only limited, non-personalised ads will be shown where permitted by law.

6. In-App Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (such as "Remove Ads" and coin packs) are processed exclusively through Google Play Billing. We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card number, bank account details, or any financial information. Purchase entitlements are stored securely on your device and verified via Google Play.

7. Data Security

All communication between the app and backend services is encrypted using HTTPS / TLS. Local game data is stored using encrypted key-value preferences on your device. While we use industry-standard measures to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

8. Data Retention

Crash and analytics data in Firebase is retained according to Google Firebase's data retention policies. AdMob may retain advertising identifiers as described in Google's privacy policy. Local game progress is retained on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

9. Your Rights & Choices

  • Opt out of personalised ads: Open your device's Settings → Google → Ads and enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization", or use the in-app Privacy Options button.
  • Reset your Advertising ID: Use your device's Google settings to reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time.
  • Disable analytics: You may limit Firebase Analytics collection by disabling usage-data sharing in your device settings.
  • Delete local data: Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in your device settings will remove all locally saved progress.
  • Request server-side data deletion: Contact us at the email below and we will delete any analytics or crash data associated with your device within 30 days.
  • GDPR / EEA users: You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, restrict processing, and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Links to Other Sites

The app may contain links to external websites or services (for example, the Google Play Store page). We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third-party sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page and, where practicable, notify users through the app. Your continued use of Arrow Escape after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
