Car Jam 3D

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This privacy policy explains how Car Jam 3D ("we", "the game") handles data and consent, including disclosures required by Google Play.

1. Consent & Privacy Flow

  1. First Launch Privacy Dialog: You choose whether to allow anonymous gameplay statistics and personalised ads, or essential gameplay only. No data is sent before your explicit choice.
  2. Consent Management Platform (UMP): In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, Google's certified consent form (IAB TCF) is displayed as required by applicable law.
  3. Ad Serving:
    • Non-personalised ads (npa=1) are served unless personalised advertising was explicitly consented to.
    • Restricted data processing (rdp=1) applies immediately if "Do Not Sell My Data" is toggled in Settings.
    • Child-directed treatment is applied if the game is flagged for general/child audience.
  4. Full Offline Playability: If all optional consent is declined, the entire game remains fully playable. Levels, coins, boosters, and garage progress require neither consent nor an active network connection.

You can adjust these settings at any time in-game via Settings > Privacy or tap Do Not Sell My Data.

2. Data Collection & Use

The game does not require user accounts, logins, email addresses, or real names. We collect zero personally identifiable information.

Data Type Collected / Shared Purpose Provider
Approximate location (IP address derived) Yes / Shared Advertising, fraud prevention Google Mobile Ads SDK
App interactions (ad impressions, taps) Yes / Shared Ad delivery and performance Google Mobile Ads SDK
Device identifiers (Advertising ID, App Set ID) Yes / Shared Frequency capping, fraud prevention Google Mobile Ads SDK
Gameplay events (levels cleared, boosters used) Optional (only if consented) Game balance & progression metrics Car Jam telemetry (no user identifiers)
In-app purchases Processed by Google Play Receipt validation Google Play Billing

3. Children's Privacy

Car Jam 3D is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the game holds no accounts or personal data on remote servers, uninstalling the app deletes all locally saved game progress from your device.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact our support team at the developer contact email listed on the Google Play Store.
